About

Experience, expertise, and a passion for people

Your Partner in HR Excellence

With 18 years of progressive experience in Human Resources, I have dedicated my career to helping organizations build strong, engaged workforces and develop HR systems that drive business success.

My journey in HR has taken me from foundational roles to the executive level, culminating in my position as Head of HR for an international company. This experience has given me unique insight into the complexities of managing human capital across diverse markets, cultures, and regulatory environments.

Professional Background

Throughout my career, I have:

  • Led HR strategy and operations for international organizations
  • Developed and implemented comprehensive talent management programs
  • Navigated complex organizational transformations and change management initiatives
  • Built HR departments from the ground up
  • Managed employee relations across multiple jurisdictions
  • Designed compensation and benefits structures that attract and retain top talent
  • Ensured compliance with evolving employment laws and regulations
  • Championed diversity, equity, and inclusion initiatives

Why Work With Me

As an independent HR consultant, I bring the strategic perspective of an executive with the hands-on practicality of someone who has worked at every level of HR. I understand that every organization is unique, and I tailor my approach to meet your specific needs, culture, and goals.

Whether you're a growing startup needing to establish HR foundations, a mid-size company navigating expansion, or an established organization seeking to optimize your HR function, I provide the expertise and guidance to help you succeed.

My Approach

I believe in:

  • Strategic Alignment: HR should be a business partner, not just an administrative function
  • People First: Sustainable business success comes from engaged, developed employees
  • Practical Solutions: Theory is great, but implementation is what matters
  • Continuous Improvement: HR practices must evolve with changing workforce expectations
